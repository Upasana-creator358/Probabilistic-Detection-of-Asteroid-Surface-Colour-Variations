# Data and Samples

## Data used in the Humes paper

The paper uses artificial observations rather than real telescope measurements. The authors created synthetic asteroids with known shapes, rotation periods, pole directions and phase functions.

For every asteroid, they produced two cases:

- a uniform asteroid with the same reflectance across its surface;
- a heterogeneous asteroid containing a large region with different reflectance in one filter.

Because the correct answer is already known, the simulations can show whether the statistical test detects a real surface feature or produces a false result.

## Samples

The simulations include different asteroid populations and observing conditions. The number of observations, measurement noise and model accuracy are changed to test the method under different conditions.

For a later real-data study, the possible inputs are:

- ZTF brightness measurements in multiple filters;
- DAMIT asteroid shape and spin models;
- asteroid-Sun and asteroid-observer geometry;
- observation times corrected for light travel time.
